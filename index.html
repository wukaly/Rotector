Reading the flags

Large block: many new lines arrived together in one save. Common for pasted resources, moved scripts, or code written in another editor.

Lines per minute: new lines divided by the time since the previous save. People rarely keep up more than a few lines a minute.

Line-by-line rewrite: roughly as many lines removed as added, in small pieces. A formatter, a mass rename, and an AI refactor all look like this, so compare the before and after code.

With Collaborative Editing, drafts can sit for hours before being committed, which makes a slow change look fast. Talk to the developer before drawing a conclusion.
